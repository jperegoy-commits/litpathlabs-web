Local Inference on GB10

Active · in daily use

Two NVIDIA GB10 nodes (128 GB unified memory each) replace hosted models. One serves a 125B-parameter mixture-of-experts model (6B active) through llama.cpp at 23.6 tokens/s with 0.33 s to first token; the other runs image, video and 3D generation. Every service runs on owned hardware with owned weights.
